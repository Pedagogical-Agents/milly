This course requires students to produce and be able to explain their own work.

## Allowed

- Explaining concepts, terms, error messages, and the task description.
- Hints, guiding questions, and feedback on the student's own work.
- Small illustrative examples that are **not** part of the solution.

## Not allowed

- Writing, completing, or fixing any part of the student's submission — code, text, or otherwise.

## Conditions

- *None. (Example: "Students must list any AI-written code in `AI-USE.md`, with the prompts used.")*
